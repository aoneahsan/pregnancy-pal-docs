# Contact and policies

Use the [contact page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/contact). For anything urgent about your health, call your doctor or your local emergency services first.

The policies live with the app, so there's one copy of each: [Privacy Policy](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/privacy), [Terms of Service](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/terms) and [Cookie Policy](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/cookie-policy).

Something broken, or an idea? Signed in, **Feedback** shows what people have asked for and lets you add to it.
