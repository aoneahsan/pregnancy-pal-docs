# Questions people ask

**I'm only planning right now. Is it for me?** Yes. Start with your periods, fertile-day estimates and questions for your doctor. They carry over if you get pregnant.

**How does it work out my week?** From the first day of your last period, or the due date your clinic gave you. Use whichever you have, and change it if your clinic moves the date.

**Does Pregnancy Pal give medical advice?** No. It keeps your record and can make suggestions from it. Only your doctor or another qualified professional can tell you what to do.

**Can my partner see everything?** Only what you share, and only after you invite them. You can take it back whenever you like.

**Do you count what I do in the app?** Yes. We count how the app is used, to improve it, and what you log stays out of those counts. The [Cookie Policy](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/cookie-policy) says exactly what we use.

**Is there an iPhone app?** Not yet. Use it in Safari.

More on the [questions page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/faq).
