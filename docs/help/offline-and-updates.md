# No signal, and updates

With no signal, you can still read your record and add quick logs: kicks, water, symptoms, medication taken and feeds. They sync when you reconnect, with no duplicates. Bigger edits wait for a connection, and a banner tells you so.

**Updates** arrive by themselves. An open tab picks up the new version on its next change of page. Now and then an update is required: the app says so, with one button to update.
