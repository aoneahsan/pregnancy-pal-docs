# Updates and renewals

Every licence and buyout includes six months of what we improve in Pregnancy Pal, counted from the day you buy.

## What counts as an update

- Fixes for anything that stops working
- Improvements to the screens and the record
- Changes when Android, browsers or our providers change underneath it
- New parts of Pregnancy Pal as we build them

## When the six months end

Your deployment keeps running on the last version it had. Only new updates stop.

## Renewing

Renewals come in 3, 6 or 12 months. A renewal counts from the later of today and the day your current updates end, so renewing early loses nothing. Renew on the [updates page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email the licence was sent to. The current renewal prices are there too.

A buyer can renew after a buyout as well, so our fixes and improvements keep reaching their copy.
