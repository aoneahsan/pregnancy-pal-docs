# White-label and licensing

A white-label licence means we run Pregnancy Pal for an organization under that organization's name. We host it, connect their domain, and put their name, logo and colours on the record. The people they look after keep the same record this site describes: periods, visits, medication, kicks, contractions and the baby's feeds.

There are two ways to have it:

- **A licence.** We run it for you. You choose the web app, the mobile app (Android and iOS), or both. No source code changes hands, and we may license it to others until somebody buys it outright.
- **The buyout.** The source code of every repository that builds what Pregnancy Pal ships becomes yours, with six months of our updates, and we stop selling it to anyone else. You pay us a royalty of 7% of the gross revenue it earns for as long as it earns, as the [licence terms](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms) explain.

Current prices are on the [white-label prices page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them from our licensing service. The plain-words licence is on the [licence terms page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms), and the full text is in the [Terms of Service](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/terms).

## Who it suits

- A clinic or maternity practice that wants the people it sees to keep readings, doses and questions in one place between visits.
- A women's health company that wants a finished pregnancy record under its own brand.
- An employer or insurer offering it as part of a benefit.
- A charity or community program supporting parents.

## Your approvals stay with you

Pregnancy Pal keeps a record and gives suggestions. It doesn't diagnose or treat, and it holds no clinical certification. If you offer it to patients, through a clinic or inside a health service, you're responsible for your own clinical, privacy and regulatory approvals wherever you offer it.
