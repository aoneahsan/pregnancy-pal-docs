# What's delivered

A licence covers the platforms you pay for. Each one is a whole platform, not a feature.

## The web app

The whole record in the browser, on your domain. It works on any phone, tablet or computer, and it's the same app as [pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com).

## The mobile app

The Android app under your name, published to your own store account. The iOS app is part of the same platform, and we deliver it once you provide or fund an Apple developer account.

## What a licence doesn't include

- **Source code.** A licence doesn't disclose or transfer any. Code changes hands only with a buyout.
- **Changes made just for you.** Those are quoted and paid for separately. Ask on the [custom work page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).
- **Third-party costs you choose,** such as your domain, store accounts and paid services.

## What the buyout hands over

- The app's source code
- The database migrations and server functions it runs on
- The Android project, ready to build and sign with your own key
- Six months of updates from the day you buy

After a buyout we take Pregnancy Pal off sale. Licences sold before it keep their terms until they end. The price is on the [buyout page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).
