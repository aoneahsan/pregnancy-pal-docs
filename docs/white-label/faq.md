# Questions about licensing

**Do we get the source code with a licence?**
No. A licence means we run Pregnancy Pal for you under your name. The code changes hands only with a buyout.

**Can someone else license it too?**
Yes, until somebody buys it outright. After a buyout we stop selling it, and licences sold before then keep their terms.

**Is there anything to pay after a buyout?**
Yes, a royalty of 7% of the gross revenue Pregnancy Pal earns, not counting sales tax or VAT you collect. You pay it every quarter with a revenue statement, for as long as it earns revenue, and it stays owed if you sell Pregnancy Pal on.

**What about iOS?**
The mobile platform covers Android and iOS. We deliver the iOS app once you provide or fund an Apple developer account.

**What happens after the six months of updates?**
Your deployment keeps running on its last version. A renewal of 3, 6 or 12 months brings the updates back. See [Updates and renewals](./updates.md).

**Can we have changes made just for us?**
Yes. Those are quoted and paid for separately. Ask on the [custom work page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

**Who handles clinical and regulatory approvals?**
You do. Pregnancy Pal keeps a record and gives suggestions; it doesn't diagnose or treat, and it holds no clinical certification. If you offer it to patients, or through a clinic or health service, getting and keeping your own clinical, privacy and regulatory approvals is your responsibility, and so is what you tell your users about it.

**Where are the prices?**
On the [white-label prices page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing), which reads them from our licensing service, so they're always the current ones.
