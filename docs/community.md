# Community

Pick a room and read. You don't have to post. A post appears with your first name and your week.

Replies come from other people at about the same stage: their experience, not medical advice. Share what happened to you, not instructions. Posts that tell people to stop or change treatment come down.

You can report a post or block someone at any time. They won't be told who did.
