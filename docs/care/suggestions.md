# Suggestions

Suggestions are patterns from your own record, worded so you can ask your doctor about them. They're suggestions, never final advice.

They stay off until you turn them on. Before you do, you see exactly what gets sent to the AI provider: the symptoms, readings and doses you've logged lately. Your name, your email and your partner's details stay out of it. You can turn it off again at any time.

Your plan includes a number of these each month.
