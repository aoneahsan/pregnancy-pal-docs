# Speak

Speak is for when your hands are full. You say what happened, we set it out as records, and you check them. Nothing joins your record until you press Save.

You can say weights, blood pressure, symptoms, kicks, water, feeds, and doses of medicines already on your list. Each draft shows the words it came from. Change a number, take out what's wrong, or leave the whole draft out.

## What happens to what you say

Speak stays off until you turn it on. Once it's on:

- Your browser may show your words as you talk, using its maker's speech service. The Android app doesn't do this.
- The sound is stored privately, sent to our transcription provider, and deleted after 24 hours.
- The words, the names of your medicines and today's date go to the AI provider to draft your records.

Your name, email, partner's details, doctor, clinic and the rest of your record stay out of it.

What we heard stays on the page until you delete it, and it's in your [export](/docs/account/your-data). Deleting it removes its sound too.

## If it can't draft right now

What you said is kept. If the AI provider is away, the draft waits and tries again by itself, and you can press Try again. If you've used this month's Speak, what you said stays there until your count starts again. Your plan sets the count: see [plans](/docs/plans).

Speak writes down what you say. It doesn't answer health questions or check your readings. If you think it's an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
