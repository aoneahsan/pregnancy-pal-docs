# Two-step sign-in

**Two-step sign-in asks for six digits from an authenticator app after you choose your Google account.** Someone who gets into your Google account still can't open your record. It's your choice, and your record works the same either way. Turn it on in **Account**, then [**Security**](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/profile/security).

Accounts that run the admin pages are the exception: those pages stay closed until two-step sign-in is on, and staff can't turn it off.

## Setting it up

1. Press **Set up two-step sign-in**.
2. Add Pregnancy Pal to an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password. Point the camera at the code, or type the setup key instead; choose "time-based" if it asks.
3. Type the six digits it shows, and press **Check the code**.
4. Keep your ten recovery codes, then press **Turn on two-step sign-in**.

If a code doesn't match, check the phone's clock is set automatically; codes change every 30 seconds.

## Recovery codes

If you lose your phone, one recovery code gets you in instead of the six digits. You get ten, each works once, and we show them only when they're made, so keep them somewhere other than your phone: printed, or in a password manager. **Copy all ten** and **Download as a file** are there to help.

Security shows how many are left. **Make new recovery codes** replaces the whole set; the old ten stop working as soon as the new ones are made. It needs a sign-in from the last 10 minutes and the six digits.

## Signing in

After Google, type the six digits for Pregnancy Pal. After five codes that don't match there's a 10-minute wait, or you can use a recovery code.

**Remember this browser for 7 days** is unticked unless you tick it. Ticked, that browser asks for Google only until the week is up; signing out of it forgets it. Use it only on a browser that's yours alone. Security lists every browser that skips the code, with **Forget** on each and **Forget every browser**.

A remembered browser still asks for the six digits to show a key or a secret, and for the admin pages.

## Losing your phone

**With a recovery code:** on the code page, choose **Lost your phone?**, then **Use a recovery code instead**. You're in, two-step sign-in stays on, and Pregnancy Pal asks you to set up an authenticator on the phone you have now. Do it straight away, so the next sign-in works. We email you that a recovery code was used.

**Without your phone or any recovery code:** write to us from the [contact page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/contact), from the email address on your account.

## What support can and can't do

To be sure it's you, we check at least two of these:

- you wrote from the email address on the account
- you named the month the account was made
- you gave the reference on your last plan receipt

We never ask about your record, your health or your dates to prove who you are.

Once two checks pass, support can reset two-step sign-in. That turns it off and forgets every browser that skips the code, and we email you straight away. Every reset is logged with the help request it came from.

Support can't reset it for an account that runs the admin pages, and can't reset a suspended account until it's restored. After a reset, turn it on again in Security whenever you like.

## A new phone, or turning it off

**Set up a new phone**, in Security, asks for a code from the phone you have now; the old phone stops working once the new one is set up.

**Turn off two-step sign-in** needs a code from your phone as well. Your recovery codes stop working with it, and any browser that skipped the code goes back to asking for Google only.
