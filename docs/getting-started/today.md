# Today, your main page

**Today** shows where you are on the tape, from before pregnancy to the months after, along with your next visit and the page you'll bring to it.

Most days the record only needs a tap. Kicks, water and doses save as you press them, even with no signal.

Write a question whenever it occurs to you. It goes first on your [visit report](../record/visit-report.md).

Every main page has a **Show me around** button at the top, for a short tour of that page.
