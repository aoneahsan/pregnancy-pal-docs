# Claiming an older record

Most accounts have already moved to the new Pregnancy Pal. A few couldn't be matched safely, so they're held, sealed, until their owner asks for them.

If we wrote to you, open the link in the email and sign in with Google. We only match the address Google has verified as yours.

After 60 days a record that hasn't been claimed goes into a locked archive. Write to us from the same Google account and we'll bring it back. The [contact page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/contact) reaches us.
