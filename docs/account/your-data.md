# Export or delete your data

Both are free on every plan.

**Export.** In **Profile**, under **Security**, you can download your whole record as a file you keep.

**Delete.** You can delete your account when you're ready, which deletes your record with it. The steps are on the [delete account page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/delete-account).

**Who can see your record.** You, and anyone you invite, one part at a time. The database itself refuses anyone else's request. The [Privacy Policy](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/privacy) has the details.
