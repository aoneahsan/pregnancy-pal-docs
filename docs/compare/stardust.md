# Moving from Stardust to Pregnancy Pal

A period, ovulation and pregnancy tracker with partner sharing and a postpartum mode. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | Stardust | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Yes | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Yes | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Partly: A postpartum mode for your own body; baby tracking isn’t mentioned | Yes |
| Community | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Export your data | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose Stardust if your cycle comes first and you want its period tracker with a partner kept in sync, on iPhone.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you need appointments, doses, kicks and contractions, and tracking for the baby afterwards.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from Stardust, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave Stardust, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [Stardust compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/stardust).
