# Moving from Nurture by Glow to Pregnancy Pal

A pregnancy app from Glow with week-by-week tracking, a contraction timer and a community of moms. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | Nurture by Glow | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Yes | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Community | Yes | Yes |
| Export your data | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose Nurture if a large community of moms is what you’re after, on iPhone.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you want kick counts, medication reminders, a partner and a report for visits, and to keep the record going once the baby’s here.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from Nurture by Glow, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave Nurture by Glow, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [Nurture by Glow compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/nurture).
