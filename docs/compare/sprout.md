# Moving from Sprout to Pregnancy Pal

A pregnancy app from Med ART Studios with a week-by-week timeline, a kick counter and a contraction timer. Its newborn and cycle apps are separate. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | Sprout | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Partly: In a separate app, Fertility & Period Tracker | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Yes | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Yes | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Partly: You can track medications; reminders aren’t mentioned | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Partly: In a separate app, Baby Tracker & Newborn Log | Yes |
| Community | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Export your data | Partly: A journal PDF, as a keepsake | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose Sprout if you want its week-by-week timeline and journal keepsake on iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you’d rather have one record than three apps, with reminders, a partner and a report for visits.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from Sprout, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave Sprout, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [Sprout compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/sprout).
