# Moving from Ovia to Pregnancy Pal

A cycle and pregnancy tracker from Ovuline, with a separate Ovia Parenting app for after the birth. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | Ovia | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Yes | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Yes | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Yes | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Yes | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Partly: In a separate app, Ovia Parenting & Baby Tracker | Yes |
| Community | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Export your data | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose Ovia if you want a free iPhone app, with no in-app purchases listed, and its more than 2,000 expert articles.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you want before, during and after in one record instead of two apps, and a report to take to appointments.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from Ovia, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave Ovia, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [Ovia compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/ovia).
