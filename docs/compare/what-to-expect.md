# Moving from What to Expect to Pregnancy Pal

The app from the What to Expect brand, from Everyday Health: week-by-week reading, a community and a free baby tracker. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | What to Expect | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Yes | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Partly: You can log medications; reminders aren’t mentioned | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Yes | Yes |
| Community | Yes | Yes |
| Export your data | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose What to Expect if you mostly want something to read each week and a big community to ask, free, on iPhone.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you need dose reminders, a report for your doctor and a partner who can see what you choose to share.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from What to Expect, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave What to Expect, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [What to Expect compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/what-to-expect).
