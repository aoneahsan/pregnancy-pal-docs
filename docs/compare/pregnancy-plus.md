# Moving from Pregnancy+ to Pregnancy Pal

A pregnancy app from Philips Digital UK: guides for each week, a kick counter, a contraction timer and a birth plan. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | Pregnancy+ | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Yes | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Yes | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Yes | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Community | Yes | Yes |
| Export your data | Partly: Birth plan only | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose Pregnancy+ if you want its week-by-week guides and a birth plan you can export, on iPhone.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you want a record that starts before the pregnancy and carries on after it, with medication reminders and a report for visits.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from Pregnancy+, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave Pregnancy+, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [Pregnancy+ compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/pregnancy-plus).
