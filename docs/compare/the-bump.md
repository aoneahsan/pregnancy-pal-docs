# Moving from The Bump to Pregnancy Pal

A pregnancy and baby app from The Knot, with an album that carries on through the first year. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | The Bump | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Yes | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Yes | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Yes | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Yes | Yes |
| Community | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Export your data | Yes | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose The Bump if you’d like a free iPhone app with a keepsake album, and CSV or PDF downloads of your information.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you also track periods or planning before a pregnancy, want dose reminders, or want a partner in on it.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from The Bump, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave The Bump, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [The Bump compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/the-bump).
