# Moving from BabyCenter to Pregnancy Pal

A pregnancy app from Everyday Health: week-by-week tracking, a kick counter, a contraction timer and a parents’ community. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | BabyCenter | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Yes | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Yes | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Yes | Yes |
| Community | Yes | Yes |
| Export your data | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose BabyCenter if you want free weekly reading, tools for late pregnancy and a community, on iPhone.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you want periods, doses and visits in the same record, and a report for appointments.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from BabyCenter, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave BabyCenter, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [BabyCenter compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/babycenter).
