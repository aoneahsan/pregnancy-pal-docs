# Moving from Clue to Pregnancy Pal

A period and cycle tracker from BioWink in Berlin, with modes for trying to conceive, pregnancy and perimenopause. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | Clue | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Yes | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Yes | Yes |
| Kick counter | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Partly: Custom reminders and logging a contraceptive method; medication isn’t named | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Yes | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Community | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Export your data | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose Clue if you’re tracking your cycle and nothing else for now, or you’re in perimenopause, which it has a mode for and we don’t.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if a pregnancy is the reason you’re here: the visit report, kick counts, contractions, doses and the baby afterwards are all in the one record.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from Clue, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave Clue, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [Clue compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/clue).
