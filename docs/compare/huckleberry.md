# Moving from Huckleberry to Pregnancy Pal

A baby tracker known for sleep, from Huckleberry Labs, with paid plans and a sleep plan you can buy. Last checked November 7, 2026.

## How they differ

| What you might need | Huckleberry | Pregnancy Pal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period and cycle tracking | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Week-by-week pregnancy | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Kick counter | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Contraction timer | Yes | Yes |
| Medication reminders | Yes | Yes: Reminders on Android |
| A report for your doctor | Partly: You can share logs with your pediatrician; a report isn’t described | Yes: On Plus and Family |
| Sharing with a partner | Yes | Yes: On Family |
| Baby tracking after birth | Yes | Yes |
| Community | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Export your data | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| Works in a web browser | Not stated on the pages we checked | Yes |
| iPhone app | Yes | No |

Choose Huckleberry if the baby’s already here and sleep is the problem you need help with. That’s its focus, and it isn’t ours.

Choose Pregnancy Pal if you want the pregnancy and the baby in one record, from before the birth to after it.

## How to move across

There's no automatic import from Huckleberry, so nothing here pretends to bring your history over.

1. Before you leave Huckleberry, check what it lets you download, and keep that file.
2. [Sign in to Pregnancy Pal](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/auth) with Google and set your dates.
3. Add your next visit and any medicine you're taking. Start logging from today.
4. If you want your partner to see part of it, [invite them](../record/partner.md).

Prices and our own plans are side by side on the [Huckleberry compare page](https://pregnancypal.aoneahsan.com/compare/huckleberry).
