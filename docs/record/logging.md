# Logging readings and symptoms

Open **Log** to add a reading after a visit, or whenever you weigh yourself. Blood pressure, weight and symptoms each keep their date and whatever you noted that day.

You see your own line, with no borrowed averages to compare yourself against. This is a record of what you entered, not a diagnosis. If a reading concerns you, speak with your doctor.

## Kicks and contractions

Counting kicks is something many doctors ask for in the third trimester. Press once for each kick. If your baby is moving less than usual, call your doctor or midwife. Don't wait, and don't rely on a counter.

Contractions are timed from the same page, and they save with no signal too, because someone in labor may not have any.
