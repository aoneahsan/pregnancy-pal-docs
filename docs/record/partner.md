# Sharing with a partner

Nothing's shared until you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time.

In **Profile**, press a capsule to move it. What's in the overlap is what your partner can see.

The invite is a link you send them yourself. Your partner signs in with their own Google account. A partner seat comes with the Family plan.
