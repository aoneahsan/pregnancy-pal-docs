# After the birth

The baby gets a section of their own: feeds, weight and length, and vaccinations.

Press a side to start a feed. The timer keeps going if your phone locks, and pressing the other side switches. The page shows the side to start on next, from your last feed. Forgot to press the timer? Add a feed by hand.

You enter what your clinic gives and measures. Schedules differ by country, so we don't tell you what's due.
