# Visits and questions

Open **Visits** for your next appointments, soonest first. You can edit one, move it, or remove it and undo that.

Add a visit with its time, place, reminder and notes. You choose when you'd like the reminder.

Write questions whenever they come to you. They go first on your visit report.
