# The visit report

The report is one page built live from your own record. Your questions go first, because they're easy to forget.

Print it, or save a PDF to send ahead. On Android, the phone's share sheet takes the file: Drive, Files, a printer or a message.

The file's name and address say nothing about what's in it, and the PDF is made on your device, so your record doesn't go to a server to make it.

The report is on the Plus and Family plans. See [plans](../plans.md).
