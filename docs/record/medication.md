# Medication

Copy the medicine and time from your prescription. We won't suggest or check an amount.

Tick each dose when you've taken it. It saves straight away, even with no signal, and it's what your visit report counts, so it's accurate about missed ones too.

The dots show the last seven days. Press a dot to fix a day you forgot to tick.

On Android, reminders arrive as notifications on your phone. They're inexact by design, so an 8:00 reminder may come a few minutes later.
