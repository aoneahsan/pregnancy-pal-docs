What’s new in the docs

These pages last changed on November 7, 2026, when the docs were rewritten for the rebuilt app. Readers can follow /feed.xml.

  • Appearance and units: One control for colours, light and dark, text size and motion. Readings are shown in the units you choose.
  • Claiming an older record: If we wrote to you about an older account, sign in with the same Google account to bring your record across.
  • Export or delete your data: Your whole record as a file you keep, and deleting your account, on every plan.
  • Exercise: Exercises for your stage with step-by-step instructions, all optional. No streaks.
  • Food: Foods grouped as enjoy, limit or avoid, what to focus on each trimester, and what you do not eat.
  • Guides: Short reads picked from your week. Calm, plain, and clear about when to call someone.
  • Suggestions: Patterns from your own record, worded so you can ask about them. Off until you turn them on.
  • Wellness and reminders: Water, posture, eye breaks, doses and visits. You choose which reminders you want and when they stay quiet.
  • Community: Small rooms by stage. Read without posting, report or block anyone, and posts telling people to change treatment come down.
  • Coming from another app: How to move to Pregnancy Pal from Flo, Clue, Ovia and other pregnancy and cycle apps, with what each offers, read from its own pages.

  • Sign in and set your dates: Sign in with Google, tell us your stage and dates, and your week is worked out from them.
  • Today, your main page: Your week, your next visit and the things you log most, in one place.
  • Contact and policies: How to reach us, and where the Privacy Policy and Terms live.
  • Questions people ask: Short answers about who it is for, plans, partners, privacy and devices.
  • No signal, and updates: What works offline, how quick logs sync, and how the app keeps itself up to date.
  • What Pregnancy Pal is: One record for before, during and after pregnancy, for you and your partner. What it keeps, what it costs and what it will not do.
  • Plans: What Free, Plus and Family each include, and where the current prices live.
  • After the birth: Feeds with a timer, weight and length, and the vaccinations your clinic gave.
  • Periods and fertile days: Log your periods and get estimates of your next period and fertile days. Estimates are labeled that way everywhere.
  • Logging readings and symptoms: Blood pressure, weight, symptoms, kicks and contractions, each with its date. Your own line, with no borrowed averages.
  • Medication: Copy what was prescribed, tick each dose, and your visit report counts what you took.
  • Sharing with a partner: Nothing is shared until you switch it on. Invite your partner with a link and choose each part they can see.
  • The visit report: One page for your doctor, built live from your own record, with your questions first. Print it or save a PDF.
  • Visits and questions: Keep each visit with its time, place, reminder and notes, and collect questions for the next one.
